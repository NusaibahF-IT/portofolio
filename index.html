Executive Case Study

SwiftBite: Q1 2026 Performance & Delivery SLA Audit

End-to-End Analytics on Financial Health, Logistics SLA, and Customer Sentiment

1. Business Context & Objective

SwiftBite operates as an on-demand food delivery platform connecting 30 merchants across 5 city zones. In Q1 2026, the platform registered 320 unique transactions representing IDR 41.57M GMV (Net Payment: IDR 38.03M). Objective: evaluate financial viability, identify courier delivery bottlenecks, and assess customer sentiment to derive actionable Q2 2026 recommendations.

2. Pipeline Architecture & Data Integrity

• Primary Source: swiftbite_pm2_integrated_order_level.csv (320 rows)
• Review Source: swiftbite_reviews.csv (190 reviews, 59.38% coverage)
• Relational Grain: 100% matched on order_id (0 row duplication, zero PII leakage)

3. Three Critical Business Findings

  • Loyal Segment Dominance (BQ-01): Loyal customers contributed 27.55% of Net Payment (IDR 10.47M) across 83 orders with highest AOV (IDR 126,229/order). Protecting retention is vital (>IDR 1M/month risk if retention drops 10%).
  • Fast Food Bottleneck (BQ-02): Fast Food delivery delay rate reached 39.58% (overall platform: 28.47%) with extreme delay peaking at 55 minutes on order O00176.
  • Delivery Delays Trigger Negative Reviews (BQ-03): 30% of Fast Food reviews were negative, primarily citing cold food and spilled packaging resulting from transit delay.

4. Strategic Recommendations & Q2 2026 KPI Targets

  1. VIP Priority Auto-Dispatch: Dispatch couriers rating >=4.8 for Loyal customers & auto-voucher if delay >15 min. (Target: Loyal retention >=95%, Net Revenue >=IDR 12M).
  2. Fast Food Radius Limit & Staging: Restrict radius to max 5 km and courier pool in busy restaurant clusters. (Target: Cut late delivery to <20%).
  3. Mandatory Insulated Thermal Bags: Enforce thermal bags for couriers & tamper-proof seals for merchants. (Target: Negative reviews <12%).
